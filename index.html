Mid-States Sync

A private, internal tool that connects Mid-States' own QuickBooks Online account to our order and inventory workflow. Not a public product.

About this application

Mid-States Sync is used solely by Mid-States Industrial Sales, Inc. and its authorized employees to manage the company's own business records in QuickBooks Online — customers, vendors, items, estimates, invoices, purchase orders, and payments. It is not offered to or used by the general public.
