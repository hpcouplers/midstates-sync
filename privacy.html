Overview

Mid-States Sync ("the Application") is a private, internal software tool operated by Mid-States Industrial Sales, Inc. ("Mid-States," "we," "us," or "our"). The Application is used solely by Mid-States and its authorized employees to manage Mid-States' own business records within its QuickBooks Online account. The Application is not offered to, marketed to, or made available to the general public or to any outside customers.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Application handles data.

Information the Application accesses

The Application connects to Mid-States' own QuickBooks Online company file through Intuit's official API, using credentials authorized by Mid-States. Through this connection, the Application may read and write business records belonging to Mid-States, including customers, vendors, items and inventory, estimates, invoices, purchase orders, and payments.

The Application accesses this information only to perform the business functions Mid-States directs — for example, creating an order confirmation, generating an invoice, or updating inventory counts.

Information we do NOT collect

  • The Application does not collect personal information from members of the public.
  • The Application does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • The Application does not use accessed data for advertising or marketing.
  • The Application does not track visitors or use analytics or advertising cookies.

How data is stored and protected

The Application authenticates to QuickBooks Online using OAuth 2.0. Access credentials are stored locally in Mid-States' own controlled environment and are never shared with outside parties. Data retrieved from QuickBooks Online is used only for the immediate business task and is not retained beyond what is necessary to perform that task.

Data sharing

We do not share data accessed through the Application with any third party, except as strictly required to communicate with Intuit's QuickBooks Online API in order to perform the requested business functions.

Your choices

Because the Application is used only internally by Mid-States on Mid-States' own accounting data, it does not process data belonging to outside individuals. Mid-States may disconnect the Application from QuickBooks Online at any time through the QuickBooks Online "Apps" settings, which immediately revokes the Application's access.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted at this URL with its effective date.
